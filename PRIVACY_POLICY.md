# Privacy Policy for NewPilot

**Effective Date:** August 4, 2026

**NewPilot** ("we", "our", or "the app") is a native, lightweight Windows 11 utility created by **GamerJagdish** that allows users to customize the behavior of the Copilot key on their keyboard.

### 1. Zero Data Collection
NewPilot does **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, usage metrics, telemetry, or diagnostic information.

### 2. Local Configuration Storage
All application preferences (such as your chosen key action, path, or hotkey preset) are saved strictly on your local device at `%LOCALAPPDATA%\NewPilot\config.json`. No configuration data is ever uploaded to external servers or third parties.

### 3. Network Access
NewPilot functions completely offline. The application makes zero network requests, tracking calls, or internet connections.

### 4. Contact Us
If you have any questions or feedback regarding this privacy policy, you may open an issue on our GitHub repository:  
https://github.com/GamerJagdish/newpilot
